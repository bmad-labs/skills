2. Purpose

The design documents are read as instructions by three readers:

  • the builder Agent, which decides what to implement and what to leave alone;
  • the planner Agent, which plans the next Story;
  • the tests, which check what a sentence promises.

A wrong sentence causes one of two faults.

FaultConsequenceExample in this batch
A sentence describes work that is not done The reader checks for an output that does not exist. A later builder assumes the work is present, so it is omitted or duplicated. {{EXAMPLE_FALSE_CLAIM — wrap the false part in …, then bold the truth}}
A sentence omits a fact that is needed The reader has no answer and must infer one. Two readers can infer differently. {{EXAMPLE_MISSING_FACT}}
